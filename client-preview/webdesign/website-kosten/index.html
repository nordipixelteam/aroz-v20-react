01 / The idea

Web Design / Strategy

Which decisions determine the effort?

Two websites with the same page count can have very different technical and design requirements. Content, components, integrations, support and approval processes determine the work involved. We separate planning, content, design, development and ongoing operation, making it clear which services are needed and where the budget goes.

Prices can be compared only when the services are comparable.
