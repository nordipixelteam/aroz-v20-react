01 / The idea

Web Design / Strategy

How does the website stay manageable after launch?

WordPress can provide a practical foundation for editorial websites. A consistent component model, sensible roles and maintainable technical implementation are essential. Instead of designing each section in isolation, we develop reusable blocks. Content should remain editable without its design falling apart at every update.

Good design stays good after the next update.
