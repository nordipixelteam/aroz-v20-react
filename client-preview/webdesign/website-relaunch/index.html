01 / The idea

Web Design / Strategy

What do we keep, and what really gets better?

A relaunch asks what already works and what prevents the next stage of development. A careless restart can lose good content and established visibility. We consider existing pages, search intent, user journeys and design together. The plan prioritises the new while protecting valuable existing content.

New does not automatically mean better.
02 / The composition

A relaunch is a controlled change.

What works is documented. What slows users down is improved. Existing URLs and content need a safe transition.

  1. 01Review before design
  2. 02URL and content mapping
  3. 03A risk-aware transition
03 / From concept to outcome

Preserve.
Improve.

We record existing strengths and risks before changing the interface, structure or technology.

  1. 01

    Take an inventory

    Record important pages, entry routes, content and dependencies before changing them.

  2. 02

    Restructure

    Improve user journeys, update outdated content and define the new design system.

  3. 03

    Switch in a controlled way

    Check redirects, technical foundations, mobile views and core functions before publication.
