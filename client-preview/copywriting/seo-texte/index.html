01 / The message

Copywriting / Strategy

What information does this page actually provide?

Search-optimised content needs to be useful first. Search intent, clarity and accuracy belong together. We distinguish overviews, detailed questions and decision support, then develop the right headings, examples and links.

Relevance comes from usefulness.
02 / What words need to do

Content follows the question, not a word count.

Copy gives direction, answers specific questions and links meaningfully to more detailed content.

Write useful answers.
