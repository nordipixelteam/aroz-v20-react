01 / Understanding

AI SEO / Strategy

Which useful answers could your content provide?

Large language models process information in context. For websites, key statements need to be precise, distinct and connected to the right topics. Instead of extending copy just to include keywords, we focus on clearly answered questions, defined terms and specific descriptions of the service offered.

A strong passage should make sense on its own.
