02 / Starting point

Go beyond information.
Show character.

For many young people, getting a driving licence is an emotional milestone. The website needed to do more than explain prices and licence categories. It had to bring the Fast Drive attitude online: personal, direct, modern and far more energetic than a traditional driving school website.

04 / Bringing the brand online

The website and brand speak the same language.

This project thrives on a deliberate contrast: AROZ provides structure and clear user journeys behind the scenes, while Fast Drive stays bold, young and distinctive at the front. The result looks like Fast Drive, rather than an agency style imposed on the brand.

05 / User journey

From first impression to registration.

A driving school website needs to answer many questions without feeling like an information archive. The content therefore follows real decisions.

01Find your bearings

What is Fast Drive, which licence categories are available and why is this the right driving school for me?

02Understand

B197, theory, practical lessons, vehicles and training are organised into clear topics.

03Build trust

The team, vehicles, learning environment and brand world give the driving school a visible, personal identity.

04Take action

Online registration and clear next steps run through the website, reducing unnecessary detours.
