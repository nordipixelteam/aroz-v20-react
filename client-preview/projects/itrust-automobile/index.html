02 / Starting point

A dealership needs to guide emotion and stock at the same time.

At iTrust, a beautiful vehicle slider alone is not enough. Users want to filter, compare, understand services, build trust and quickly find the next step. The website needs to be a brand presence, vehicle platform and gateway to services at once.

Design principlePremium vehicle presentation at the front. Clear search, service and contact logic underneath.
