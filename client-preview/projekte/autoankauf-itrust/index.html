02 / Starting point

When selling a car, variety is not the priority. Confidence is.

People selling a car usually have the same questions: what is my vehicle worth? Is the process trustworthy? How quickly will I receive payment? The website needed to resolve these uncertainties step by step rather than hide them.

Design principleRather than showing everything, guide users from doubt to a clear action as directly as possible.

04 / Building trust

Conversion works only when users trust the process.

01Free valuation

The website makes it clear early that the valuation and offer are free and carry no obligation. This lowers the barrier to starting.

02A transparent process

Vehicle details, offer, viewing and payment are explained as a clear sequence rather than a black box.

03Credibility

The offer communicates personal advice, objective valuation and BVfK membership as a basis for trust.

04Immediate payment

The most important endpoint stays visible: payment follows handover immediately, by instant bank transfer or cash.
