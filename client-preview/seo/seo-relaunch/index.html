01 / The real question

SEO / Strategy

Which SEO signals need to be preserved?

A relaunch often changes URLs, content and internal links at the same time. Without a review, users and search engines lose important guidance. Before the change, we map relevant pages to their new destinations. Redirects are only part of the plan: content, status codes and internal journeys matter just as much.

The move is complete only when every route works.
