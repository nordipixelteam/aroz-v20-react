SEO / Strategy

What purpose does each individual page serve?

A good SEO structure organises topics, creates clear connections and answers real search needs. Isolated keyword lists do not make a compelling website. We consider topics, search intent and information hierarchy together, so service pages, questions and examples build meaningfully on one another.
