01 / Basic principle

Visibility comes from Structure.

For a website to become visible, its content needs a clear structure. Pages should follow a logical order, topics need to connect and content needs to be understandable.

SEO therefore begins with the website's structure rather than isolated measures.

Why SEO matters for websites.

For many businesses, the website is a central point of contact for potential customers. Search engines often determine whether the website is found at all.

03 / Website structure

02 / Content

Topics need to connect.
